## Fixes Applied

### P1 — Misfiled article: type/directory/index-section mismatch (3 issues in one)

`jepa-rl-humanoid-grasping.md` declared `type: open_question` but lived in `wiki/concepts/` and was listed under `## Concepts` in `_index.md`, while `wiki/open_questions/` sat empty. It was also the wiki's only **orphan** (0 inbound links) despite linking out to 10 articles, and its `sources:` field was empty — the one frontmatter violation in the wiki.

Fixed together:
- Moved to `wiki/open_questions/jepa-rl-humanoid-grasping.md` (the directory its own `type` calls for).
- Rewrote its 5 same-directory concept links to `../concepts/…`; the 5 `../papers/…` links were unaffected by the move.
- Moved its `_index.md` entry from `## Concepts` to `## Open Questions`; updated its path in `_summaries.md`.
- Populated `sources:` with the 5 raw PDFs it synthesizes (`rlt.pdf`, `V-JEPA2.pdf`, `V-JEPA-AC.pdf`, `dex_func_grasp.pdf`, `pi_06_experience.pdf`).
- Added backlinks (See Also + `related:`) from the 4 articles it most depends on: `joint-embedding-predictive-architecture.md`, `latent-world-models.md`, `vision-language-action-models.md`, `xu_2025_rlt.md`.

### P1 — Contradiction: eigengrasp DOF count

`jepa-rl-humanoid-grasping.md` §5 claimed "9-dim PCA over 20-DOF hand, **following Agarwal et al. 2023**" and "reduces the dexterous action space from ~20 to 9 dimensions". Both `papers/agarwal_2023_dex_func_grasp.md` and `concepts/dexterous-manipulation.md` correctly report Agarwal et al. running PCA on a **16-DOF** (LEAP) hand to get 9 components. The attribution was wrong.

Fixed: the claim now reads 9-of-16 attributed to Agarwal et al., with a `> **Verify:**` note that §5's own ~20-DOF humanoid-hand assumption needs its own PCA — 9 components should not be carried over unexamined.

### P2 — Broken links (2): LaTeX parsed as markdown

`papers/schulman_2017_ppo.md:90` and `concepts/policy-gradient-methods.md:75` wrote PPO's entropy bonus as `S[\pi_\theta](s_t)`. The `](` sequence makes any markdown link checker — including `tools/lint.py` — read it as a link to a file named `s_t`. These were **false positives in substance but real defects in form**: they would break any link-aware renderer or graph view (e.g. Obsidian).

Fixed by rewriting the term as `S\big[\pi_\theta(\cdot\,|\,s_t)\big]`, which renders identically in intent, is arguably more precise about what the entropy is taken over, and contains no `](`.

### P3 — `related:` field style inconsistency (5 articles)

42 articles used directory-prefixed paths (`concepts/foo.md`); 4 used bare filenames (`foo.md`) that resolve against no directory, and 1 used a YAML block list instead of an inline array. Bare names made 21 `related:` entries unresolvable by path.

Normalized to the majority convention (inline array, directory-prefixed) in: `concepts/learning-from-human-videos.md`, `concepts/visual-affordances-robotics.md`, `papers/bahl_2022_whirl.md`, `papers/bahl_2023_vrb.md`, `open_questions/jepa-rl-humanoid-grasping.md`. All 47 articles' `related:` and `sources:` targets now resolve to existing files.
